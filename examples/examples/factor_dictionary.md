# Factor families

The research table contains signal metadata, exit and forward-return labels, and factor families including:

- **Trend structure:** moving-average spreads, slopes, price above/below medium- and long-term averages.
- **Compression and repair:** moving-average entanglement, cross counts, and time since the last compressed regime.
- **Volume:** relative volume, pre-signal volume maxima, turnover proxies, and post-compression bursts.
- **Momentum:** Wilder RSI, CCI, MACD histogram state, expansion, and zero-line recovery.
- **Price behaviour:** prior returns, volatility, gap size, range width, breakout state, and proximity to recent highs.
- **Market regime:** SPY moving-average state, 5/20-day return, trend slopes, regime label, and a rule-based risk score.

Raw licensed market data is intentionally excluded from the public repository.
